Supported Targets

Build for All 5 Major Operating Systems

Compile once and distribute everywhere. Generate native packages ready for direct distribution or official app stores.

Android

.apk & .aab

Direct APK for instant testing and Google Play Store App Bundle (.aab) with custom Keystore signing.

Apple iOS

.ipa & Xcode

Production IPA binaries for sideloading and full Xcode project export ready for TestFlight & App Store.

Windows

.exe & NSIS

High performance desktop executable running Chromium WebView2 with full hardware acceleration.

macOS

Linux

Core Features

Everything You Need for Enterprise Apps

Turn standard responsive web apps into full-fledged native desktop and mobile applications without writing native code.

Camera & Microphone Access

Native Android & iOS permissions are automatically configured so your WebRTC video streams, photo captures, and mic recordings work seamlessly.

One-Click Cloud Engine

Powered by GitHub Actions workflow runners. No heavy Android SDKs, Flutter tools, or Xcode installations required on your local machine.

Custom Splash & Icons

Upload your brand logo and splash screens once. ieWebNative generates all density assets across Android mipmaps and iOS AppIcon catalogs.

Offline Cache & Storage

Code Signing & Keystores
